05 / Before we begin

A few good questions.

Can we start with just one service?

Yes. Start with a brand identity, a website, a clearer lead process or social media support. We can discuss that scope without bundling in everything else.

Do you work with existing brands?

Yes. You don’t need to start from scratch. We can discuss what is working, what needs to change and how the new work should fit your existing business.

How are pricing and timelines decided?

Brand and website work can be scoped as projects; content, social and advertising can be discussed as ongoing engagements. Share your priorities, timing and budget context. Deliverables, revision stages, responsibilities and any ongoing support are agreed in the proposal before work begins.

What does CRM work include?

We start with how enquiries arrive, who follows up and where leads get lost. The scope can then cover pipelines, workflows, integrations and automations around those needs.

What happens after I enquire?

Your enquiry starts a conversation about your business, goals and fit. We use that information to clarify the scope and next steps. If your brief is still taking shape, tell us what you know and we can start there.
