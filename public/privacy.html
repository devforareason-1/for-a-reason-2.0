Privacy, in plain language.

This notice covers project enquiries made to For a Reason through this website.

What you choose to share

The enquiry form asks for your name and at least one reply channel: email or phone. You may also share your business name, service interests, budget context and a project brief. Please do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.

How enquiries reach us

If direct submission is available, the form sends the details you enter to our Google Apps Script endpoint for storage in a Google Sheet. The form confirms receipt only after the endpoint acknowledges the save. Until that connection is active, the form prepares a draft for you to review and send through WhatsApp or your email app; preparing a draft alone sends nothing.

Opening WhatsApp or an email application passes your prepared brief to that service. Its own privacy terms apply. Public portfolio links open Instagram, which is a separate service.

Why we ask

We use enquiry details to understand your project, contact you about it and manage that conversation. Supplying a budget or choosing a service does not place an order or create a booking.

Storage and your choices

The form keeps an unsubmitted brief in the current page; it does not intentionally save it to browser local storage. Submitted or sent details may remain in the receiving Google Sheet, email or messaging service. Contact us if you would like to ask about access, correction or deletion of your enquiry.

Website technology

This website does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Fonts and portfolio preview images are served with the site. The hosting provider may process normal request data to deliver the website. External services you choose to visit operate separately.

Questions?

Email connect@forareason.in about your enquiry or this notice.
